Critical issue found and fixed during this work

While checking how homeowner data moves through the system, we found that a logged-in buyer could get a homeowner's name, phone number and address without paying, through an internal address that should never have been reachable from the internet. It is closed in two places (the internal address is blocked, and it no longer returns personal data), and tests now guard it. As a precaution we recommend reviewing the server access logs for this address, which we can do on request.

B. Business decisions

30 audit findings, grouped into 14 questions
1. How long can a buyer open a dispute?FirstThe prototype says 48 hours after purchase. The app uses "until 1 hour after the appointment".

This affects dispute screens, emails and when sellers get paid.

We suggest: keep "until 1 hour after the appointment time" (current behaviour), using the appointment's local time.
F-POL-01 · D-POL-01
2. When does an unsold appointment stop being for sale?FirstToday an unsold appointment stays for sale for 48 hours after its start time.

Buyers can currently buy an appointment whose time has already passed.

We suggest: stop selling at the appointment start time. The seller can relist it with a new time.
F-RISK-02 · D-RISK-02
3. What does a buyer get when they win a dispute?FirstMarketplace credit, a cash refund, or both? And who can approve each?
We suggest: marketplace credit by default (current behaviour). A cash refund to the card only when a super admin approves it with a written reason.
F-ADM-05 · D-ADM-03
4. What can support staff do compared with super admins?FirstToday both admin roles can do almost everything.
We suggest: Support can view everything, handle disputes and flag listings. Only super admins approve or reject sellers, suspend accounts, change the reserve policy, issue cash refunds and export audit logs.
F-ADM-01 · F-ADM-04 · F-ADM-06 · D-ADM-01 · D-ADM-05
5. What must a seller finish before listing?FirstSigned agreement, identity check, call recording, do-not-call/TCPA attestation.
We suggest: a signed agreement and passed verification before any listing, a confirmation call recording before a listing goes public, and a TCPA/DNC attestation renewed every 12 months.
F-SELL-READY-01/02 · F-CONSENT-02 · D-SELL-READY-01 · D-CONSENT-01
6. How much of a seller's earnings do we hold back, and for how long?SoonThe reserve policy screen exists, but payouts don't use it yet.
We suggest: new sellers (first 30 days) 10% held 14 days, sellers with more than 5% disputes 20% held 30 days, established sellers 0%. Super admins can move a seller to a stricter tier for up to 90 days, with a reason.
F-OPS-01 · F-ADM-03 · D-OPS-01 · D-ADM-02
7. How do we catch the same lead listed twice?SoonToday a repeated lead from a CRM creates two appointments.
We suggest: same homeowner phone and same address within 30 days means "possible duplicate". It is held as a draft for the seller to confirm, not published automatically.
F-RISK-01 · D-RISK-01
8. Which time zone is an appointment in?SoonTimes now show exactly, but in the viewer's time zone. Payout release uses UTC.
We suggest: use the time zone of the appointment's ZIP code, show it on every screen and email ("Tue, Oct 6, 11:40 AM CT"), and release seller funds at end of day in that time zone.
F-TIME-01/02 · D-TIME-01/02
9. Who earns a "Top rated" badge?SoonThe landing page shows "Top Seller" at a 4.5 rating, which you haven't approved.
We suggest: an average of 4.5 or higher, from at least 5 ratings in the last 90 days, with no lost disputes in the last 30 days. Recalculated daily.
F-MKT-01 · F-TRUST-02 · D-MKT-01 · D-TRUST-01
10. Tax documents and payoutsBefore launchIs a W-9 required before payouts? Who issues 1099s and monthly statements?
We suggest: let Stripe Connect collect tax details during payout onboarding and issue the 1099 forms, and block payouts until Stripe marks the account complete. Monthly statements come from the ledger as a download on the Earnings page.
F-FIN-COMP-01/02 · D-FIN-COMP-01/02
11. Legal texts, privacy and account deletionBefore launchLaunch states, Privacy Policy, Terms, cookie consent, deleting data on request.

These need your legal counsel. We build the screens and record exactly which version each user accepted.

We need: the launch states, final Terms, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy, and your retention rule for closed accounts (we suggest 7 years for financial records and 90 days for everything else).
F-COM-01 · F-DATA-01 · F-POLICY-01/02 · D-COM-01 · D-DATA-01 · D-POLICY-01/02
12. Notifications and supportLaterWhich messages are mandatory, who writes the email copy, where support requests go.
We suggest: purchase, dispute and payout messages are always sent by email, new-lead alerts follow each user's settings, and your team owns email copy with our review. Support is a shared inbox at support@homigos.com for launch, with a helpdesk tool later.
F-NTF-01 · F-ADM-04 · F-PII-03 · F-SUP-01 · D-NTF-01 · D-ADM-04 · D-PII-03 · D-SUP-01
13. CRM integrations and listing reportsLaterWhich CRMs first, prices in dollars or cents, how buyers report a bad listing.
We suggest: start with your two most-used CRMs and ask for a sample export from each. Prices are in dollars unless the source says otherwise. Buyers can report a listing with a reason, and it goes into the admin moderation queue.
F-ING-01/02 · F-MOD-01 · F-MKT-04 · D-ING-01/02 · D-MOD-01 · D-MKT-03
14. Security and data-retention rulesLaterAPI key expiry, service-to-service credentials, how long raw CRM data and documents are kept.
We suggest: seller API keys expire after 12 months with a reminder, raw CRM data is deleted after 90 days, document download links expire after 5 minutes, and audit logs are kept for 2 years.
F-SEC-02/03 · F-OPS-02 · F-PII-02/04 · D-SEC-01/02/03 · D-OPS-02 · D-PII-01/02 · D-ADM-05
